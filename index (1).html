Three recent builds, up close.

A restaurant, an online shop and a landing page. Each one was designed around a different business goal, and each one is live today.

Sakina's BBQ

Sakina's BBQ is a premium Indian grill house in Karen, Nairobi, where aromatic spice meets the open flame. I created a multipage website that showcases everything about the restaurant, from its signature grills and curries to its story and atmosphere, in a way that is as beautiful and engaging as the food itself. Every page is designed to make visitors hungry and make it easy for them to book a table.

Visit sakina-bbq2.vercel.app

A dynamic hero. The homepage opens on a panoramic strip of the grill's signature dishes, from charred kebabs to glazed wings and spiced prawns, so visitors can almost taste the food before they read a word. Refined lettering and two clear buttons send people straight to the menu or to directions.

Stories told through photos, video and reviews. Each section pairs rich food photography with short, inviting copy about the grill, the curries and the people behind them. I added videos of the dining room so guests can feel the atmosphere before they arrive, an about section that tells the restaurant's story, and guest reviews that build trust ahead of a first visit.

The full menu, online. I brought the restaurant's complete menu onto the site so guests can browse every dish before they visit, then finished each page with a clear invitation to reserve a table, alongside opening hours and directions.

Homage Essentials

Homage Essentials supplies professional and household cleaning products to homes, hotels and businesses. I built them a fully functioning ecommerce website where customers can browse the entire range, choose their sizes, fill a basket and check out, all while keeping the premium navy and gold identity the owners had already built for their brand.

Visit homage3.vercel.app

True to the owners' brand. Rather than reinventing the business, I carried its existing identity through every page: the navy and gold crest, the elegant serif lettering and the real product photography. The site feels like a natural extension of the brand customers already know and trust.

The entire catalogue, organised. I added the owners' full product range and grouped it into four clear collections: laundry, personal, surface and specialty care. Customers find what they need in a couple of taps instead of scrolling through one long list.

Built for the way people buy. Every product offers each size the business sells, from 2 litre bottles to 20 litre drums and refills by the litre, each with its own price and quantity controls. Households and commercial buyers can order exactly what they need.

A working cart and checkout. I built a basket that slides in beside the catalogue, keeps a running subtotal and lets customers remove items as they go. Checkout asks for a Safaricom M-Pesa number, so paying fits the way Kenyan customers already pay.

Bidii Plumbing

Bidii Plumbing Solutions is a Nairobi practice covering tap installations, pipe repairs, leak detection and water heating, and they needed a professional presence online, fast. I designed and built a focused landing page that tells customers what Bidii does and gets them in touch. It shows that a simple page, done well, can make a real impact, and that it can be ready in less than a day.

Visit bidii-mojz.vercel.app

Clear from the first second. A bold headline, one sentence covering every service and two obvious buttons mean visitors know who Bidii is and what to do next. Small badges for opening hours, M-Pesa payment and dispatch answer the questions customers ask most, before they have to ask.

Everything on one page. Services with their specifications, a link to the full catalogue and a direct enquiry form all sit on a single, easy scroll. There is nothing to get lost in, which is exactly why a landing page like this can be designed, built and launched in under a day.

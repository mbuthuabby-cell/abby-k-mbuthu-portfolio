Selected work

Three recent websites, and the thinking behind them.

A restaurant, an online shop and a landing page. Each was designed around a different business goal, and each is live today.

01 Restaurant website, Karen, Nairobi

Sakina's BBQ

A multipage website for a premium Indian grill house, designed to make visitors hungry and make booking a table easy.

Visit the live site

What the business needed

A website worthy of the food. Sakina's wanted guests to see the grills, curries and atmosphere online, understand the story behind the restaurant, and book a table without friction.

What we designed and built

A multipage site with Home, About, Gallery and Contacts pages. The homepage opens on a panoramic strip of signature dishes. Further sections pair food photography with short copy about the grill, the curries and the people behind them, with video of the dining room and guest reviews.

Why we made these design choices

  • Food first. The photography leads before any copy, so the page sells the product in the first second.
  • Two buttons only. "Explore the menu" and "Find us" cover what first time visitors most want to know.
  • Restrained lettering and white space. A quiet, refined type treatment lets the colour of the food do the work.
  • Trust before the visit. Video and guest reviews help a new guest feel confident before they arrive.

What we implemented

  • Multipage site navigation
  • Full menu browsable online
  • Video of the dining room
  • Guest reviews section
  • Table reservation prompts
  • Opening hours and directions

What we learned and would improve

Showing the complete menu mattered more than expected, because guests decide before they visit. The menu is currently shown as images of the printed boards, so the next step is converting it to real text, which helps search engines, screen readers and slow connections.

02 Online shop, cleaning supplies

Homage Essentials

A fully working online shop where homes, hotels and businesses can browse the range, pick sizes, fill a basket and check out.

Visit the live site

What the business needed

Homage supplies professional and household cleaning products to homes, hotels and businesses. They needed to sell the whole range online, to very different buyers, without losing the premium navy and gold identity they had already built.

What we designed and built

An ecommerce site with Catalogue, About, Services, Blog and Contact pages. The full product range is grouped into four collections. Each product page lets customers choose a size, set a quantity and add to a basket that slides in beside the catalogue.

Why we made these design choices

  • Extend the brand, do not replace it. We carried over the navy and gold crest, the elegant serif lettering and the real product photography.
  • Four collections. Laundry, personal, surface and specialty care get customers to the right product in a couple of taps.
  • Every size on one card. Sizes from 2 litre bottles to 20 litre drums, plus refills by the litre, each with its own price and quantity control, so households and commercial buyers order exactly what they need.
  • A slide in basket. Customers keep browsing while they build an order, and the subtotal is always in view.

What we implemented

  • Catalogue of four collections
  • Per size pricing and quantity controls
  • Add to cart and remove from cart
  • Running subtotal in the basket
  • Safaricom M-Pesa number at checkout
  • Product cards with size selection

What we learned and would improve

Matching an existing brand is harder than inventing a new one: spacing, contrast and type all have to feel like the original. Pricing by size also taught us how much clarity a simple stepper control adds compared with a dropdown. Next, we would add automatic payment confirmation so orders are confirmed without a follow up message.

03 Landing page, plumbing services, Nairobi

Bidii Plumbing

A focused landing page that tells customers what Bidii does and gets them in touch, designed, built and launched in under a day.

Visit the live site

What the business needed

Bidii Plumbing Solutions covers tap installations, pipe repairs, leak detection and water heating. They needed a professional presence online quickly, and a simple way for customers to reach them.

What we designed and built

A single scroll landing page: bold hero, a short practice introduction, services with their specifications, a link to the full catalogue and a direct enquiry form. An "Enquire now" button sits in the header.

Why we made these design choices

  • One goal. Every section points towards an enquiry, so visitors are never left wondering what to do next.
  • Answer questions early. Badges for opening hours, M-Pesa payment and dispatch cover what customers ask most, before they have to ask.
  • Water toned imagery with a bright blue accent. It feels clean and trustworthy, and makes the buttons impossible to miss.
  • A serif headline with an italic twist. It gives a local trade business a distinctive voice without hurting readability.

What we implemented

  • "Enquire now" button in the header
  • Two clear hero calls to action
  • Services with specifications
  • Direct enquiry form
  • Link through to the full catalogue
  • Hours, M-Pesa and dispatch badges

What we learned and would improve

A tight deadline is a useful design tool: with a single goal and one page, every element had to earn its place. Using AI for first drafts of layout and copy, then refining by hand, is what made a one day turnaround possible without looking rushed. A future version could add customer testimonials and photos of completed jobs.
